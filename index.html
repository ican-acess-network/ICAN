About us

Expanding Access to Global Higher Education

We are a global coalition of trusted, high-quality college access organizations that identify and prepare talented students from low-resource backgrounds to study at universities outside of their home country. Through this mission, we support each other by sharing best practices and support universities by providing context and information on students from organizations in our network.

How do we support each other?

We facilitate peer learning on student identification, selection, advising, preparation, student success, and ethical engagement with universities among our members.

We are a unified voice for college access organizations that support low income international students, a population that is underserved and faces unique challenges within higher education.

How do we support universities?

We serve as an organized point of engagement for universities recruiting talented, low-income international students from abroad and provide context on applicants from our regions of operation.

We increase transparency, trust, and efficiency in international student recruitment and support.

Frequently Asked Questions

ICAN membership is open to non-profit or mission-driven organizations that identify and support high-achieving students from low-resource backgrounds to access undergraduate education outside their home country. Eligible organizations must:

  • Be a registered non-profit or mission driven organization.
  • Run a competitive selection process.
  • Provide college access advising or mentorship.
  • Demonstrate a commitment to ethical engagement and student success.
  • Serve primarily first generation and or low income students.
  • Not be a traditional diploma granting "schools".
  • Be nominated by a current member of ICAN.

Yes. ICAN offers two membership types:

  • Executive Members: Founding organizations invited to co-create ICAN's structure, strategy, and standards.
  • General Members: Organizations admitted through a formal application and review process after ICAN's launch.

Organizations can join ICAN through a structured process that includes nomination and a formal application. Applicants must provide details about their mission, selection and advising model, team, and student outcomes. Membership decisions are based on alignment with ICAN's values, quality of programming, and capacity to contribute to the network.
