About ICAN

We are a global coalition of trusted, high-quality college access organizations that identify and prepare talented students from low-resource backgrounds to study at universities outside of their home country.

What drives us

Our Mission

Through shared best practices, strategic coordination, and collective engagement with universities and education partners, ICAN expands access and improves outcomes for high-achieving, low-income international students.

What we stand for

Core Objectives

Six commitments that guide everything ICAN and its member organizations do.

Advocate

Amplify the voices and aspirations of underserved students worldwide who possess extraordinary potential but lack access to higher education pathways.

Share Best Practices

Facilitate peer learning among member organizations on student identification, selection, advising, preparation, and ethical engagement with universities.

Coordinate with Universities

Serve as a credible, organized point of engagement for universities recruiting talented, low-income international students from abroad.

Leverage Collective Scale

Negotiate shared benefits with the College Board, Common App, testing agencies, and service providers on behalf of all member organizations.

Set a Quality Bar

Act as a stamp of approval for the most rigorous, ethical, and effective international access organizations globally.

Strengthen the Ecosystem

Increase transparency, trust, and efficiency in international student recruitment and support across the global higher education landscape.

Africa

KenSAP

338 Scholars
237 Alumni

About

KenSAP provides access to world-class higher education for gifted, needy Kenyan high school graduates who would otherwise have no chance to pursue such an opportunity. Most of KenSAP students come from simple, rural backgrounds that would be utterly unrecognizable to their North American classmates, and many are from families that even by Kenyan standards count as disadvantaged.

Selection & Support

KenSAP selects its students — 15 to 20 each year — through a lengthy, rigorous process that considers their academic attainments, family background, extra-curricular accomplishments and other personal qualities. The selected students then take part in 15 weeks of residential training consisting mainly of preparation for the American SAT exams and guidance through the complex university application process. The training also includes a general orientation to American culture and the unfamiliar academic conventions of American universities.

Beca Cometa

107 Scholars
20+ Universities

About

Beca Cometa is an initiative by the Intercorp Group that aims to expand access to high-quality higher education for talented Peruvian students from low-income backgrounds. The program focuses on supporting students with strong academic performance and leadership potential, helping them pursue undergraduate studies in the United States. By investing in education, Beca Cometa seeks to contribute to the long-term development of Peru and improve opportunities for future generations.

Selection & Support

Beca Cometa follows a highly selective, multi-stage admissions process that evaluates applicants based on academic achievement, English proficiency, socioeconomic background, and personal competencies. The process includes psychological, socioeconomic, and academic evaluations across five elimination phases. Around 60 students are selected for Beca Cometa Camp, an intensive preparation program that provides SAT training, English certification support, college application guidance, essay advising, and emotional counseling. Successful participants may then receive the full Beca Cometa, which funds tuition, living expenses, travel, and other essential costs for undergraduate studies in the U.S.

Duolingo University Access Program

25 Current Scholars
12+ Universities

About

The Duolingo University Access (DUA) Program, created in partnership with Duolingo and UNHCR, aims to expand access to higher education for refugee students worldwide. The program addresses the global gap in educational access, where only a small percentage of refugees are able to attend university, by providing structured pathways to top institutions. Through its efforts, DUA supports the broader goal of increasing refugee enrollment in higher education and creating more equitable academic opportunities across countries.

Selection & Support

The program identifies high-potential refugee students and provides individualized, comprehensive support throughout the entire university application journey. This includes one-on-one advising, assistance with financial aid applications, English testing through the Duolingo English Test, and guidance through complex immigration and visa processes. Students benefit from a "wrap-around" support model, which also includes interview preparation, transition support, and funding assistance when needed. The program works closely with UNHCR offices and operates in multiple regions, supporting students who go on to enroll in universities across the United States, Canada, and other countries.
