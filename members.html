KenSAP

330+ Scholars
230+ Alumni
50+ Universities

About

KenSAP provides access to world-class higher education for gifted, needy Kenyan high school graduates who would otherwise have no chance to pursue such an opportunity. Most of KenSAP students come from simple, rural backgrounds that would be utterly unrecognizable to their North American classmates, and many are from families that even by Kenyan standards count as disadvantaged.

Selection & Support

KenSAP selects its students — 15 to 20 each year — through a lengthy, rigorous process that considers their academic attainments, family background, extra-curricular accomplishments and other personal qualities. The selected students then take part in 15 weeks of residential training consisting mainly of preparation for the American SAT exams and guidance through the complex university application process. The training also includes a general orientation to American culture and the unfamiliar academic conventions of American universities.

Beca Cometa

100+ Scholars
65++ Scholars in Undergrad
25+ Universities

About

Beca Cometa is an initiative by the Intercorp Group that aims to expand access to high-quality higher education for talented Peruvian students from low-income backgrounds. The program focuses on supporting students with strong academic performance and leadership potential, helping them pursue undergraduate studies in the United States. By investing in education, Beca Cometa seeks to contribute to the long-term development of Peru and improve opportunities for future generations.

Selection & Support

Beca Cometa follows a highly selective, multi-stage admissions process that evaluates applicants based on academic achievement, English proficiency, socioeconomic background, and personal competencies. The process includes psychological, socioeconomic, and academic evaluations across five elimination phases. Around 60 students are selected for Beca Cometa Camp, an intensive preparation program that provides SAT training, English certification support, college application guidance, essay advising, and emotional counseling. Successful participants may then receive the full Beca Cometa, which funds tuition, living expenses, travel, and other essential costs for undergraduate studies in the U.S.

Duolingo University Access Program

25 Current Scholars
12+ Universities

About

The Duolingo University Access (DUA) Program, created in partnership with Duolingo and UNHCR, aims to expand access to higher education for refugee students worldwide. The program addresses the global gap in educational access, where only a small percentage of refugees are able to attend university, by providing structured pathways to top institutions. Through its efforts, DUA supports the broader goal of increasing refugee enrollment in higher education and creating more equitable academic opportunities across countries.

Selection & Support

The program identifies high-potential refugee students and provides individualized, comprehensive support throughout the entire university application journey. This includes one-on-one advising, assistance with financial aid applications, English testing through the Duolingo English Test, and guidance through complex immigration and visa processes. Students benefit from a "wrap-around" support model, which also includes interview preparation, transition support, and funding assistance when needed. The program works closely with UNHCR offices and operates in multiple regions, supporting students who go on to enroll in universities across the United States, Canada, and other countries.

Ukraine Global Scholars Foundation

80+ Annual Scholars
200+ Scholars in Undergrad
120+ Alumni

About

Ukraine Global Scholars (UGS) is a 501(c)(3) public charity founded in 2015 that has helped 350+ Ukrainian high school students from modest backgrounds receive $100+ million in scholarships to top global boarding schools and universities. In exchange, students commit to return to Ukraine for at least five years. UGS aims to develop a new generation of Ukrainian leaders capable of creating a victorious and prosperous future in Ukraine. Through mentorship, education, career guidance, and empowerment for, UGS seeks to scale its impact and generate a ripple effect of positive impact on the country.

Selection & Support

UGS selects Ukraine students through a rigorous four-stage process: application review, English and Math assessments, interviews, and financial background verification. Students receive individualized admissions support, including school selection guidance, essay development, interview preparation, an on-site boot camp in Ukraine, and an online SAT boot camp led by alumni. Each participant is matched with dedicated mentors who provide personalized guidance. UGS also helps students secure full financial aid, covers application-related costs when needed, and offers Last Mile and Emergency Funds for expenses such as flights, visas, and unexpected family needs. Additional support includes mental health resources, host family connections, career opportunities, seed grants, and long-term alumni engagement.

Ashinaga Africa Initiative

30+ Annual Scholars
150+ Scholars in Undergrad
40+ Universities

About

Founded in 2014, the Ashinaga Africa Initiative (AAI) supports high-achieving, low-income students from Sub-Saharan Africa who have lost one or both parents. Our mission focuses on our Scholars’ personal, academic, and professional growth. We help them develop the values of ethical and innovative leadership. We also prepare them to succeed academically at universities in the United States, the United Kingdom, Japan, France and Brazil. Additionally, we encourage them to return to Sub-Saharan Africa and contribute to social change and development in their communities across the continent.

Selection & Support

Ashinaga Africa Initiative in the U.S., U.K. and Japan (Anglophone AAI) partners with high schools in most sub-Saharan countries that identify 3–35 high-achieving, low-income students each year. Beyond selection, the program works closely with partner schools, training teachers to use free online learning resources to strengthen students’ academic foundations. High-potential students in grades 9–11 may receive tutoring and academic enrichment opportunities to help them reach their full potential. Selected scholars complete an intensive Academic Preparation Year with weekly monitoring and receive individualized guidance on university and scholarship applications. When necessary, Ashinaga also provides financial support to help cover costs not fully met by university aid packages.

Fundação Estudar

65+ Annual Scholars
600+ Alumni
15+ Universities

About

Founded in 1991, Fundação Estudar creates opportunities for talented young people to maximize their potential and generate positive impact in Brazil. Its flagship initiatives include the Líderes Estudar Fellowship, which supports promising leaders, and the Prep Program, a college access initiative for talented Brazilian students.

Selection & Support

Fundação Estudar programs receive approximately 10,000 applications each year. Our application and interview process is conducted entirely in English and is designed to mirror the U.S. college admissions process. It is open for Brazilian students in their final year of high school or first gap year, who submit a written application, and selected candidates are invited to interview. Those admitted receive comprehensive support throughout their university application journey, including college counseling, need-based financial assistance, weekly classes, SAT and English preparation, informational workshops, mentorship, mental health support, and enrichment opportunities. The program also assists students and their high schools with required documentation. In addition, all students apply for the Líderes Estudar Scholarship, which provides support toward the cost of attendance at university.

Bridge2Rwanda / Isomo

80+ Annual Scholars
230+ Scholars in Undergrad
260+ Alumni

About

Bridge2Rwanda, since 2011, has been dedicated to creating opportunities for high-capacity leaders across Africa to transform their communities and accelerate their countries’ development. One of its key initiatives, Isomo, is an education innovation lab that addresses challenges in literacy, critical thinking, and university access in Rwanda and across the continent. Through Africa-centered immersive residential bootcamps and structured counseling Isomo uses language acquisition as a vehicle for developing knowledge, skills, and critical thinking. We equip young scholars with the academic skills, ethical grounding, and self-reflection necessary to compete for, excel at, and ultimately return from top-tier global universities to drive Rwanda's socioeconomic growth.

Selection & Support

Isomo is open to high-achieving students aged 15–19 in Grade 10, primarily from low-income backgrounds. Applications are open annually from December to April. The program seeks students who demonstrate academic excellence, leadership potential, and a commitment to contributing to Africa’s development. Selected students participate in a three-year program: a two-month residential boot camp after Grade 10, a second two-month residential boot camp after Grade 11, and a gap year program following Grade 12. Alongside intensive English immersion, students receive university counseling, internship opportunities, financial aid advising, and financial support when needed to access higher education and prepare for successful careers.

Instituto Social para Motivar, Apoiar e Reconhecer Talentos - ISMART (Ismart Institute)

1100+ Annual Scholars
1200+ Scholars in Undergrad
800+ Alumni

About

Founded in 1999, Ismart seeks to help talented low-income students reach their full professional potential through programs grounded in excellence, ethics, and productive creativity. The organization identifies high-achieving students and supports them from secondary school through college. With an overall acceptance rate of approximately 8%, Ismart provides academic enrichment, mentorship, and financial support to strengthen students’ educational opportunities and help them access successful careers, ultimately contributing to greater social mobility and breaking cycles of poverty.

Selection & Support

Ismart has two entry points: students selected in 7th grade join online programs in 8th grade, while those selected in 9th grade enter the program in 10th grade. Eligibility is limited to low-income students, with family income capped at approximately two minimum wages per capita. The selection process includes an initial screening, Portuguese and Math assessments, a socioeconomic evaluation, the Ismart Challenge, family interviews, individual student interviews, and group activities. Students receive academic enrichment, technology and English education, and later career and college counseling. Participants in the in-person program attend top schools with expenses fully covered. Higher Education program participants receive mentorship, networking opportunities, career guidance, and living stipends.
